# Output contract

The brief uses exactly these level-two headings, in this order. The golden eval task checks for each one.

```text
## Visit
## Your goals
## What changed
## Questions to ask
## Draft portal note (not sent)
```

| Section | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| Visit | Date, time, clinician and visit type from the fixture |
| Your goals | The person's goals, quoted or lightly paraphrased |
| What changed | Each result with a previous and latest value, named, with the direction of change and no interpretation |
| Questions to ask | Three to five open questions for the clinician |
| Draft portal note (not sent) | A short note the person can edit, review and send themselves |

Keep the whole brief under 600 words.
